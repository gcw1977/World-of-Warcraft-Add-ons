# GatherLedger roadmap — 1.x

## v1.0.0 — Stable baseline
Version-only promotion of v0.6.17. Same addon behavior, SavedVariables, and interface.

## v1.1.x — Gathering statistics
Compute probabilities and uncertainty from successful gathering observations. Show sample sizes prominently; require 100 observations for a creature/species-level recommendation.

## v1.2.x — Additional gathering professions
Reuse the existing gathering record and shopping architecture for mining, herbalism, and fishing. Verify profession-specific event detection and item/source attribution instead of assuming all use the skinning event path.

## v1.3.x — Source recommendations
Search materials and rank credible sources without inventing gold/hour or gathering time estimates.

## v1.4.x — Recipe queue
Track planned crafts individually to edit or remove projects rather than merely subtracting aggregated reagents.

## Beyond
Consider separate WoW Forever support when its APIs and data are verified.
