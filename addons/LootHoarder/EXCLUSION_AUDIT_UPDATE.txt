LootHoarder v0.2.3 — Exclusion audit

- /lh exclusions opens a scrollable audit of excluded gathering windows.
- Includes observed time, source (if identifiable), spell/reason, location, item offers, and whether a money slot appeared.
- Retains only the latest 60 exclusion records; older detailed records are evicted.
- Old exclusion counters remain but cannot be reconstructed retroactively.
- The four-second heuristic alone no longer excludes windows when the evidence is too weak.
- A recent Skinning cast with a corpse or recent gathering with no identifiable corpse remains classified as an inferred exclusion and is flagged as such.
- No stored loot totals are reset; no loot is automatically discarded.
- Live in-game verification is needed, particularly for skinning, fishing, and shared loot sources.
